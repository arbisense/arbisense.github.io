# Arbisense
### Making Sense of Humans

---

## About Us

**Arbisense AB** is a Swedish SME, established in 2022, dedicated to providing innovative solutions for evaluating human behavior through scientifically validated technology. We bridge the gap between health science and engineering by specializing in:

* **Passive Sensing:** Utilizing smartphone technology to gather behavioral insights.
* **Large-Scale Data Collection:** Managing extensive datasets efficiently.
* **Big Data & AI Analytics:** Turning raw data into actionable intelligence.

We provide end-to-end solutions, closing the loop by offering professional smartphone and web application development alongside secure cloud deployment.

---

## Our Services

We provide ready-to-use, adaptable solutions for living environment sensing on a large scale. Our core product is the **Arbisense GeoEnv platform**, designed for researchers, policy makers, and community engagement.

### 📱 Smartphone-Based Data Collection
Our platform enables remote and unobtrusive data gathering. Key features include:
* **Multimedia:** Collection of photos and annotations.
* **Location Tracking:** GPS location capturing with robust anonymization and privacy protection.
* **Questionnaires:** Flexible delivery of pre-planned or just-in-time questionnaires (Ecological Momentary Assessment).
* **Ideal For:** Real-world studies requiring high user engagement.

### ☁️ Secure EU-Based Cloud Storage
We prioritize privacy and compliance:
* **GDPR Compliance:** Secure cloud services located entirely within the EU.
* **Privacy First:** Supports large-scale data collection without jeopardizing participant privacy via open cloud services.

### 📊 Advanced Data Analytics & Visualizations
Our flexible, Python-based GeoEnv dashboard offers:
* **Customizable Views:** Tailored access for policy makers, educators, health scientists, and clinical personnel.
* **Rich Visualizations:** Map presentations, dynamic filters, and combinatory data views.
* **Integration:** REST API support for integrating results into other platforms.

## Featured Projects

Arbisense contributes technology to impactful health and environmental studies across Europe.

* **PREVENT:** An EU-funded action addressing childhood obesity to reduce cancer risk factors. Arbisense employs technological tools in living labs to identify gaps in prevention policies.
* **CONNECT-CARE:** Transforming healthcare with personalized obesity prevention strategies using AI to predict and prevent diseases at an early stage.
* **In Your Phone:** A study on children's exposure to digital food marketing. We provided tools for children to submit screenshots of ads, objectively measuring exposure.
* **In Your Face:** A "citizen scientist" project where children mapped their urban foodscape using location-aware photography.
* **FESKIS:** Exploring the food environment at Karolinska Institutet's southern campus using our *Mobile Sense* app to log meals and analyze eating patterns.
